## About this game

**Every cheat you buy is one less thing you have to do.**

You spawn in a chunky voxel arena with a free pistol and bots that shoot back. Kills pay cash. Cash
buys cheats from a sketchy injected mod menu: Speed Hack, Infinite Ammo, Auto Bhop, Wallhack, No
Recoil, Triggerbot, Hitbox Expander, Aimbot, Loot Magnet... and, if you get banned enough times,
God Mode, Spinbot and Noclip.

Every cheat levels up from "barely noticeable" to "absolutely broken". The aimbot starts as a tiny
circle around your crosshair and ends up covering the whole screen.

- **A cheat ladder, not a skill tree:** 12 cheats, each removing a chore, each with its own
  upgrade path from weak to absurd.
- **CS-style rounds:** freeze time, a buy menu with guns from pistols to an LMG, and a gun you keep
  until you die.
- **Real bhop:** Counter-Strike-style air strafing, or let Auto Bhop time your jumps for you.
- **Bots built to need cheats:** snipers hide at range (Wallhack), rushers strafe (Aimbot), and
  later arenas pay ten times more and hit ten times harder.
- **Get banned to go further:** prestige resets your run but pays Hax Points for permanent
  Rootkit perks. God Mode, Spinbot and Noclip only unlock after bans.
- **The chat teaches you:** no tutorial pop-ups; a shady seller DMs you hints and bots react to
  how obvious you are.
- **Toggle off for a legit bonus:** play clean and kills can drop extra coins.

*Single-player only. All "cheats" are in-game features; this game contains no real hacks.*
